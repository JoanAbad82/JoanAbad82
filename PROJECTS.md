1. Production repositories and research-intake repositories are separate trust domains.
2. A research proposal is not evidence of a production change.
3. Historical plans/specifications can be useful context but may be non-authoritative; prefer repository-local canonical-source guidance when present.
4. Tests, schemas, manifests, release artifacts, and traceability files are stronger verification surfaces than profile prose.
5. A missing license field should be treated as legally unspecified, not inferred.
